# Recurring personas

The MTC's "progressing investigator" analog: the AI plays the same fictional
person across sessions, with their state saved here and pasted into the header
when used. Familiar characters + progressively harder questions = cumulative
roleplay without model memory. Update the state lines after each appearance.
